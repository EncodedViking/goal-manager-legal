# Goal Manager Privacy Policy

**Last updated: October 15, 2026**

Goal Manager ("the app", "we", "us") is a personal goal-planning application built and run by an
individual as a hobby and learning project. It is not a commercial service. This policy explains what
information the app collects, why, and the choices you have.

## What we collect

- **Account details:** your username, password (stored only as a salted hash, never in readable form),
  and, if you choose to give them, your first and last name, email address and mobile phone number.
- **Your content:** the goals, milestones, tasks and daily habits you create in the app.
- **Communication preferences:** whether you agreed to receive text messages and emails, and the date
  and time you agreed. We keep this as our record of your consent.
- **Technical data:** standard server logs (such as time of request and IP address) used to keep the app
  running and to diagnose problems.

## How we use it

- To run the app and show you your own goals and tasks.
- To send you the messages you opted in to (see below), and nothing else.
- To keep the app secure and fix problems.

## Text messages (SMS)

If you tick the box agreeing to receive text messages and give us a mobile number, we will text that
number with messages about your Goal Manager account: goal and task reminders, progress updates, and
account notices such as test messages you request. We do not send advertising or promotions.

- **Consent is optional** and is not a condition of using the app.
- **Frequency varies** with your goals and settings; expect only a few messages per day at most.
- **Message and data rates may apply**, depending on your mobile plan.
- **To stop** receiving texts, reply **STOP** to any message, or untick the box on the Account page. Reply
  **HELP** for help, or contact us using the details below.
- **No sharing of mobile information:** we do not sell, rent or share your phone number, or your text
  messaging opt-in and consent, with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.
  Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties.

## Who we share information with

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with service providers needed to run the
app:

- **Twilio**, which delivers text messages on our behalf and receives your phone number and the message
  text for that purpose. See [Twilio's privacy notice](https://www.twilio.com/legal/privacy).
- **Anthropic**, whose Claude model powers the app's optional AI planning features. If you use them, the
  text you type into them, and the goal and task details needed to answer, are sent to Anthropic. See
  [Anthropic's privacy policy](https://www.anthropic.com/legal/privacy).
- **Hosting providers** that store the app's data.

We may also disclose information if the law requires it.

## Email

If you tick the email box and give an email address, we may email you about your account and goals. Use
the unsubscribe instructions in any email, or untick the box on the Account page, to stop.

## Retention and your choices

We keep your information while your account exists. You can view and edit your details, and change your
communication choices, on the Account page at any time. To have your account and data deleted, contact
us using the details below.

## Security

Passwords are hashed, and access to the app requires you to sign in. No system is perfectly secure, so
we cannot guarantee absolute security.

## Children

The app is not intended for anyone under 16, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

## Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above. Material changes to how we use your phone number
will be made only with your consent.
